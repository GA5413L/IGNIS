## 2. Normal-gravity companion tests (UC Berkeley, Link et al.)
- Black PMMA rods, d = 1.27, 0.95, 0.64, 0.32 cm. V_g = 25 or 2 cm/s. X_O₂ 17.0–20.9 %. Each tabulated value is an **average of ≥ 3 repeats**; the repeats themselves are not published. Methane-flame ignition; flow increased to blow-off; steady ≥ 5 min or 4 cm ([L18] p.4; [L18-ESM] Table S2, 46 rows, of which 9 print "-" for both V_f and ±).
- Fig. 6b data (1–500 cm/s) are **not tabulated** (C-22).

## 3. BASS-II thin-sheet critical-velocity tests (Bhattacharjee PI)
- Thin PMMA films (e.g. 100 µm and 200 µm, 1–2 cm wide) in opposed flow. V_g,cr summarised in [TM] Table 2.1 for 21 % and 16 % O₂ at 1 atm (Evidence Case 01). Test matrix in [TM] Table A.1 (partially read). **Its labels B1, B10…B22 collide with Link's rod labels** (C-14).

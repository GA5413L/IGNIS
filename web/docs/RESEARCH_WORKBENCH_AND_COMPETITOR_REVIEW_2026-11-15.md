## Research that changes the priorities

The FY2026 NASA [Residence Time Driven Flame Spread task report](https://taskbook.nasaprs.com/tbp/index.cfm?TASKID=2413F182B9F30E0CDB221036094EFF0F&action=public_query_taskbook_content), updated March 2026, describes more than 1 TB of images, sensor records and telemetry, synchronization using ignition signals, and multi-camera tracking using MATLAB and the Python Flame Tracker application. It extracts flame position/length/area and compares observations with theory. This is direct evidence for investing in measurement pipelines and traceable comparisons. It is not evidence that NASA has requested or evaluated IGNIS.

[NASA-STD-7009B](https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-7009) addresses intended use, model credibility and program-approved acceptance criteria. [NIST's FDS quality-assessment publication](https://www.nist.gov/publications/quality-assessment-fire-dynamics-simulator-bridge-reliable-simulations) treats verification/validation and application reliability as engineering work. Neither source certifies this project. [NIST's residual-analysis guidance](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section4/pmd44.htm) explains why a scalar fit score alone is inadequate; a plot can expose structure hidden by a mean. Our generic channel difference is not automatically a model residual or a validation test.

The [2025 PIML combustion review, v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03347) covers physics constraints in kinetics and reacting-flow learning. The abstract and metadata were inspected; this is not a replication or full-paper synthesis. Physics-informed ML is a legitimate future approach, but it does not supply missing independent measurements, calibration or a validated target.

| Intersecting field | Useful engineering requirement | Current decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire chemistry / low-temperature oxidation | Preserve fuel, oxidizer/diluent, pressure and the measured endpoint; visible flame loss is not every form of extinction. | Keep source-specific findings and endpoint audit; no universal learned extinguisher or safety score. |
| Gravity / transport / ventilation | Separate buoyancy, imposed flow, geometry and wall effects; partial gravity is not an interpolation label. | Retain applicability limits and missing conditions. No gravity-adjusted prediction added. |
| Radiation / solid pyrolysis / thermal thickness | Distinguish material/geometry, boundary heating and derived versus independently measured quantities. | Preserve supplied-paper dossiers and exact nominal-design audit; do not infer run data from aggregate plots. |
| Metrology / optical tracking | Declare operator, calibration, clock, alignment and upstream processing; retain raw witnesses. | Add same-operator channel differences now. Calibrated video ingestion is the next substantive gap. |
| Statistics / experimental design | Keep run/specimen/campaign dependence, missingness and chosen model class explicit. | Descriptive within-run arithmetic now; no invented confidence intervals or pooled training claim. |
| ML / deep learning | A specified target, leakage-safe grouped split, simple baseline, error analysis and external validation. | No new physical predictor before cutoff. Build/evaluate one narrow model when suitable run-level data are available. |
| Scientific data engineering | Versioned inputs, source identity, immutable raw bytes and recomputation. | API metadata checks, existing source locators, replay packages and figure code. |
| Quantum computing / broad aerospace analogies | A demonstrable computational task and relevant physical model. | No documented need for quantum computation in these current tasks. Public spacecraft/rocket research does not establish relevance to low-velocity cabin-fire prediction by itself. |

## Statistical capability and the owner's competitiveness concern

Several competitors have implemented regression, ML or optical extraction. IGNIS did not have a predictive benchmark and remains behind on those dimensions. The new immediate contribution is **cutoff-free descriptive rank analysis** on actual SAME-R cells: scatter (AW) versus ionization delta (BK), 108 paired rows from 109 spacecraft-material rows. Pooled Spearman rho is +0.44358, while Silicone is -0.61847 across 22 pairs; deleting any one of its 14 recorded sample labels keeps the sign negative, from -0.66775 to -0.54607. Kapton is near zero (+0.02365, 29 pairs) and its deletion range crosses zero. This asks a useful research question without converting repeated observations into independent trials. These are assistant-observed source patterns, not a claim of a novel physical discovery. Inspect raw voltage ranges and instrument status before assigning a mechanism or interpreting tiny ranked differences.

The browser has linked raw/rank plots, flight/material tables, every deleted group, source-cell inspection, complete JSON export/recheck and downloadable Python arithmetic/figure code. The TypeScript CLI rechecks the current pinned source; a separate Python implementation reproduced 281 coefficients from exported cells. No p-values, confidence intervals, causal effects, calibrated detector-performance ranking or trained physical predictor is reported. [Method and exact scope](DETECTOR_ASSOCIATION_METHOD_2026-11-15.md), [default result](../artifacts/refinement/detector-association.json), [figure](../artifacts/refinement/detector-association.png).

A regularized FLEX termination-category model is a plausible next predictive baseline after endpoint/lineage adjudication and group-held-out evaluation. A hierarchical model needs credible run/specimen/calibration groups; a measured-versus-model residual study needs synchronized series currently absent from our Saffire collection. Deep learning is not supported merely by having hundreds of correlated rows. [Current priority plan](WIN_PLAN.md).

## Implemented technical improvements

**Interactive research figures:** first-click material isolation and subsequent multiple-material toggling now work in four Figure 2 panels and the statistical scatter. Surviving points retain their positions and axes. Statistical summaries retain their complete analysis population; the legend controls display only. Visible SVG/JSON downloads carry source-row identities, missingness counts and the exact view recipe, while static publication downloads remain labeled full/default. This closes a concrete interaction/reproduction gap without claiming feature exclusivity. [Visibility contract](FIGURE_VISIBILITY_2026-11-15.md).

**Channel differences:** `/console#local-data` now compares two explicitly selected columns within one run and phase. Quantity, units and measurement operator must match; a linear/additive scale and common reference/datum are explicitly asserted alongside clock/context and upstream alignment. Differences are left minus right. The plot links to raw rows. The mean, mean absolute and root mean square differences use equal weights per available row; missing pairs remain visible. Repeated/decreasing times or incompatible declarations block aggregates; arithmetic overflow blocks an aggregate instead of silently dropping an extreme pair. One-pair descriptive values are defined, but do not establish uncertainty or generalization. Scaled unsigned summaries avoid overflow from squaring; signed accumulation preserves small cancellation residuals. Unrepresentable statistics return an explicit unavailable value and warning. Relative lag and upstream processing can still change the differences. Raw-data inclusion requires explicit export consent. Replaying the package recomputes both source inspection and differences. Origin/operator/alignment declarations are not independently verified. No fit, offset adjustment, conversion, interpolation, hypothesis test or model execution occurs.

**Citation identity:** seven fixed existing identifiers are checked through five NASA NTRS records, one Crossref DOI and one DataCite DOI. All seven returned matching identifiers and normalized titles on this pass. The dated receipt includes endpoint, HTTP status and metadata hash. No arbitrary URL, model-generated citation, private text or key is submitted. This checks registration/title identity, not claim entailment, full-text availability, retractions or scientific correctness. Other catalogue sources remain outside this API snapshot. ## Winning strategy and remaining gaps

The best demonstration is a reproducible chain: show the pooled versus flight-stratified detector reversal; inspect the exact rows/missing denominator and cutoff sensitivity; change the model question from later remote CO2 to chemical heat release; then export/recheck the source-bound result. Follow with an explicitly hypothetical local comparison to show how mismatched operators are blocked. A polished 3D demonstration can support geometry understanding later, but cannot substitute for the missing calibration/validation work.

The strongest category fit remains **Best Use of Science** and **Best Use of Data**; **Best Use of Technology** is a stretch dependent on demonstrated usefulness and integration. This is strategy, not a predicted award. The official challenge/judging web pages returned 502 during this refresh; category context relies on the previously inspected organizer PDF. Ten global awards do not mean ten slots for this challenge.

Before claiming adoption readiness, obtain a researcher-led reproduction of a real task, compare errors/time against their current workflow, and publish an independently reviewed source/measurement mapping. The largest gaps are researcher validation, calibrated image ingestion, broader BYOD formats, an accepted SI interpreter and external validation of any future predictive model. Training a model tonight would not close those gaps.

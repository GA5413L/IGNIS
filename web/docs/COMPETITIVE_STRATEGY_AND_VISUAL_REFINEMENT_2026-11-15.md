# IGNIS: competitive position, useful visuals and the next research contribution

15 November 2026. Fourteen priority public repository heads refreshed at about 10:56 UTC (15:56 PKT), six competitor interfaces opened in a browser, and a public-research review followed by a critical discussion with Claude. This extends the earlier 37-repository audit and 282-project screen; it is not a new exhaustive inspection of every entrant. Private projects and future changes remain outside the evidence.

**Assessment:** IGNIS is not established as the best overall entry. VESTA, MARGIN and Kenyalang communicate their purpose more immediately; Fireline offers a useful image-analysis workflow that IGNIS lacks. IGNIS has substantial measurement/claim discipline and a concrete descriptive result, but no comparative researcher study establishes its advantage. A professional interface must make that result understandable, not merely expose more controls or disclaimers.

## Where 3-D earns its place

| Visual | Useful research task | Admission requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Oxygen–pressure–gravity plot | Show separate gravity regimes and gaps; switch to aligned 2-D views for exact comparisons. | Real coordinates with definitions, source context, missing values and clear treatment of categorical/not-to-scale gravity. Proximity is not equivalence. |
| Apparatus / specimen viewer | Inspect confinement, exposed faces, sensor positions, flow direction and which quantity each instrument measures. | Published geometry/locators; unknown dimensions remain unknown; distinguish schematic from CAD and CFD. Keep an accessible 2-D view. |
| CFD field viewer | Inspect computed temperature/species/velocity fields and compare probes with observations. | An actual solver output, mesh/boundary conditions, source terms, model version and quantity-specific verification/validation. |
| Spacecraft or flame illustration | Explain mission context and improve engagement. | Explicit illustration label; no invented temperatures, safety verdicts or implied simulation. |

[NIST FDS](https://pages.nist.gov/fds-smv/) computes low-speed fire-related flow; Smokeview displays its results. [ParaView](https://www.paraview.org/about/) supports scientific analysis/visualization pipelines. NASA's [Saffire VI modeling paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20250002675) describes PyroSim geometry/model preparation, supplied payload histories, earlier heat-transfer calibration and remote-sensor comparisons. The lesson is to connect geometry, inputs, measurements and claims. An attractive field rendering is only one part of that workflow.

## Recommended product and scientific contribution

Build a **research workbench for claims that can be inspected and challenged**: source → measurement → selected population → assumptions → sensitivity → qualified finding → reproducible handoff. The target user is preparing or reviewing a combustion comparison. The intended useful output is a defensible comparison record and a precise description of the evidence still needed.

The existing SAME example provides a concrete centerpiece. Over independent [1,3] V cutoff ranges, the exact jointly finite differences are pooled [66,78]/133, flight 1 [−6,−3]/25 and flight 2 [72,81]/108. Across [0,5] V, the pooled difference can take either sign. These are descriptive finite-data results from stored workbook cells, not alarm calibration, detector superiority, causal effects or new fire physics. Unknown configuration and source-identity issues remain attached. [Implementation and source context](PROFESSIONAL_RESEARCH_AND_IMPROVEMENTS_2026-11-15.md), [NASA PSI-101](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-101).

**Build .7 refinement:** the worked example puts the computed plot and finding alongside a concise introduction, offers pooled/flight views, displays both offered presets' pooled outcomes together, keeps the measurement boundary visible and moves exact values/source details into the next section. The landing page now leads to that demonstration. The plot is descriptive analysis, not a simulated field. Canonical data, mathematical method, contracts and export semantics are unchanged. Exports preserve the selected recipe; a display toggle is not another scientific selection or a record of every exploratory click.

**Next substantive module:** a sourced five-lane view of a published model: supplied input, calibration, initialization, observation operator, comparison target. Link each lane to its source and to the exact claim it can support. Add the apparatus viewer only where sourced geometry resolves a real question. The current W1–W3 contracts and Data & methods page are a foundation, not a complete implementation of this proposed visual module.

**Later:** calibrated image/time-series import, source/run ancestry reconciliation, independent source review and a measured comparison with an equally informed spreadsheet. No all-competitor superiority, SOTA algorithm, independently novel scientific discovery or NASA adoption is established.

## Intersecting fields, with a purpose for each

This is a task-focused map, not a claim to have exhausted every discipline or paper.

| Field | Relevant variables / decision | Product consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Fluid mechanics and gravity | Buoyancy, forced flow, acceleration history, residence time, confinement. | Preserve flow direction/profile and gravity regime; do not interpolate Earth-to-Moon behavior from a slider. |
| Atmospheres and ECLSS | Oxygen fraction/basis, absolute pressure, diluent, temperature, ventilation and species histories. | Separate coordinate gaps and measurement histories. Equal oxygen partial pressure does not establish equivalent burning. |
| Heat transfer | Radiation, conduction, thermal thickness, emissivity, wall/cargo exchange. | Record prescribed/calibrated parameters and the quantity to which they apply. |
| Materials / polymer science | Grade, additives, thickness, faces, char/melting, backing and prior burns. | Material labels alone are insufficient joins. Cohort restrictions precede comparisons. |
| Chemical kinetics | Mechanism/rate choices, transport, low-temperature oxidation and reignition. | A future Cantera task needs a defined mechanism and validation domain; visible flame disappearance is not complete extinction. |
| Aerosols and detection | Particle distributions, aging/dilution, line losses, optical/ionization operators and calibration. | Keep sensor voltage, particle quantities and alarm endpoints separate. |
| Suppression and recovery | Delivery geometry, agent/fuel coupling, endpoint and cleanup. | No universal dose or “best extinguisher” inference from unrelated experiments. |
| Metrology / time-series analysis | Units, baseline, clocks, measurement windows, uncertainty and censoring. | Synchronized paired observations must be established; spreadsheet row equality is insufficient. |
| Statistics and mathematics | Dependence, finite bounds, sensitivity, partial orders and identifiability. | Exact descriptive bounds are useful now; confidence intervals require a justified sampling model. A coordinate Pareto frontier is not an optimal experiment plan. |
| Machine learning | Label definition, campaign splits, leakage, calibration, abstention and external validation. | Train only after defining an answerable predictive task and a meaningful baseline. Software tests and pooled accuracy are insufficient. |
| Experiment design | Which measurement would distinguish competing explanations, with real costs/constraints. | Plan a test that resolves a named uncertainty; do not label arbitrary geometric coverage as information gain. |
| HCI / visualization | Comprehension, source lookup, useful completion, error and task time. | Evaluate the visual workflow on actual research tasks, including cases requiring a supported answer rather than abstention. |
| Research software / open science | Data versions, DOIs, rights, executable environment and review status. | Preserve source/method identity and inspectable interfaces. A checksum establishes identity, not truth. |
| Quantum chemistry | Molecular energy surfaces, rate constants and spectroscopy upstream of mechanisms. | Relevant to mechanism development; no demonstrated need for a quantum-computing feature in this dashboard. |

The source bibliography and dataset locators are in the existing [professional-tool research](lab/11_professional_tools_2026-11-15.md), [data opportunities](lab/12_data_opportunities_2026-11-15.md), [ML assessment](lab/13_ml_decision_2026-11-15.md) and [cross-field synthesis](RESEARCH_LAB_SYNTHESIS_2026-11-15.md). New checks reinforce rather than erase those constraints:

- [Thomsen et al., PMMA mixed-convection study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20200000557): the abstract specifies 10 mm PMMA, normal-gravity pressures 40–100 kPa and 200 mm/s flow, with a Saffire-II comparison. This is a bounded published correlation worth a later model-use card. We did not reproduce it or admit a general transfer model from its abstract.
- [Cantera governing equations](https://cantera.org/stable/reference/onedim/governing-equations.html): specified reacting-flow equations and assumptions belong with any chemical calculation; it is not a complete spacecraft fire model.
- [NASA-STD-7009B](https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/nasa/nasa-std-7009): model credibility and acceptance depend on the intended use and program criteria. IGNIS is not claiming compliance or NASA acceptance.
- [NASA PSI update](https://science.data.nasa.gov/features-events/physical-sciences-informatics-updates): persistent identifiers, metadata and reusable data are a real institutional priority. Link to original investigations; do not replace PSI with an unverifiable duplicate.

Promising next acquisitions remain synchronized Saffire series, calibrated BASS imagery, SAME run/aging lineage and NIST measurement references. They are not automatically one compatible training dataset. See the acquisition report for what is already held and what still needs reconciliation.

| Award | Fit / requirement for a stronger case |
|---|---|
| Best Use of Science | **Primary fit.** Defensible methods, clear limitations, independent review and a meaningful research task. |
| Best Use of Data | **Primary fit.** Trace NASA data through an accessible, reusable analysis and show what a user can accomplish. |
| Best Storytelling Award | **Secondary fit.** One memorable measured-data finding, an understandable interaction and a concise demo. |
| Art & Technology Award | **Secondary/stretch.** A distinctive visual language with useful interactions; polish alone is not enough. |

| Galactic Impact | **Longer-term potential.** Evidence of a significant use case and credible route to benefit; no adoption is presumed. |

| Most Inspirational | **Possible, unproven.** Depends on the human story and demonstrated accessibility/engagement. |
| Global Connection Award | **Weak current evidence.** Source sharing is not evidence of connecting a global research community. |
| Local Impact Award | **Weak current evidence.** No tested local fire/research use case establishes community benefit. |

The strongest strategy is a coherent Science/Data entry with excellent presentation, rather than claiming to fit every award. The challenge's SI-powered interpretation remains an important gap: runtime interpretation has not passed the existing evaluation/acceptance process. UI polish does not close it. The release/submission dependencies also remain in the completion plan.

# Additional competitors and current IGNIS comparison

15 November 2026, PKT. This research compares public pinned source with IGNIS build `web-2026-11-15.2`, registry `2026-11-15.r2`. It does not rerun competitors' scientific models or call their AI services. The integrated conclusions are in [the current comparison](../COMPETITIVE_COMPARISON_AFTER_BUILD_2026-11-15.md).

FlameBound, MicroFire Atlas and Flamefall substantially raise the baseline for IGNIS. Source-level provenance, guarded interpretation, research-gap explanations and useful exports already exist. IGNIS's more defensible contribution is its specific treatment of measurement identity, missing readings, compatible comparison groups and changed model-use claims. Superiority on those tasks still needs a common evaluation.

## FlameBound

Pinned repository: [`a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac`](https://github.com/prattaybanik03/flamebound-flame-in-freefall/tree/a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac).

Its matching engine is substantive: five declared weighted components, exponential coordinate distances, separate recorded-data coverage, original cells and units, missing-value statuses, discrete-level handling and exclusions for planned envelopes. These are heuristic retrieval choices rather than calibrated physical probabilities. The source explicitly separates ranking from its oxygen and gravity warnings. Flow direction and thickness are displayed but not scored; the observed UI says so. [Ranking and warning code](https://github.com/prattaybanik03/flamebound-flame-in-freefall/blob/a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac/web/src/lib/bcms.ts).

The product encodes scenario inputs in a validated URL and supplies printable/Markdown reports. That is a practical advantage over IGNIS's current in-memory workspace. Its source preserves 23 investigation records and 1,980 table records with source columns, row locators and per-value status, while acknowledging that a table record is not necessarily an individual experiment. [URL implementation](https://github.com/prattaybanik03/flamebound-flame-in-freefall/blob/a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac/web/src/lib/url.ts), [data method](https://github.com/prattaybanik03/flamebound-flame-in-freefall/blob/a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac/docs/DATA.md).

At 06:04:51 PKT, the public scenario URL rendered HTTP 200 with no page exceptions: PMMA, 34% oxygen, 56.5 kPa, 20 cm/s and microgravity. It displayed Saffire-II 2-7 as the best flammability match at 68.6%, an 11.9 percentage-point oxygen gap, unreported pressure and an extrapolation warning. The page presents its coverage finding against its ingested PSI corpus. This does not negate the later Saffire literature incorporated in IGNIS and MARGIN. The browser inspection did not request an AI briefing. [Live scenario](https://flamebound-flame-in-freefall.vercel.app/?pillar=flammability&o2=34&kpa=56.5&flow=20&dir=concurrent&mat=pmma&g=0), [captured view](../../../_raw/competitive_deep_2026-11-15/public_ui/flamebound.png).

Two distinct interpretation guards deserve separate assessment. Brief validation checks that citation IDs exist, numbers occur somewhere in the entire evidence packet, and selected prohibited phrases are absent. That packet also contains target values and calculated scores; passing the check is weaker than proving each number came from its cited NASA record. Question answering has a stronger per-sentence cited-passage number and quotation check. Neither implements general semantic entailment. This is a source inspection, not a live exploit or measured failure rate. [Brief guard](https://github.com/prattaybanik03/flamebound-flame-in-freefall/blob/a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac/web/src/lib/brief.ts), [answer guard](https://github.com/prattaybanik03/flamebound-flame-in-freefall/blob/a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac/web/src/lib/qa.ts).

Its seven acceptance scenarios are executable specifications of intended app behavior. The twelve retrieval questions explicitly served tuning as well as evaluation, so they are development checks rather than an untouched benchmark. This disclosure is a strength. Original code has an Apache 2.0 license. [Acceptance cases](https://github.com/prattaybanik03/flamebound-flame-in-freefall/blob/a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac/web/src/lib/validation.ts), [retrieval questions](https://github.com/prattaybanik03/flamebound-flame-in-freefall/blob/a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac/web/src/lib/askEval.ts), [license](https://github.com/prattaybanik03/flamebound-flame-in-freefall/blob/a7adbbeb05f3c4cd6af3b7141c91e8ed09d8d9ac/LICENSE).

## MicroFire Atlas

Pinned repository: [`acb9aaa69486154da657c61469d3a1886e4c12b1`](https://github.com/A-K-M-Asifuzzaman/MicroFire-Atlas/tree/acb9aaa69486154da657c61469d3a1886e4c12b1). It shares an owner with MicroFire V2; these repositories are not evidence of two separately registered teams.

Atlas is a serious research-workflow comparator as well as an educational product. Its planning engine deduplicates exact scenarios, distinguishes observed BASS/Saffire/LUCI records from planned FM2 context, derives hypothetical candidate conditions and estimates which registered condition gaps those candidates could cover. It explicitly excludes geometry, scale, duration, feasibility, cost and predicted safety impact from that calculation. Five export types include corpus and source-manifest hashes. IGNIS does not currently implement an equivalent multi-question planning workflow. [Planner](https://github.com/A-K-M-Asifuzzaman/MicroFire-Atlas/blob/acb9aaa69486154da657c61469d3a1886e4c12b1/apps/web/lib/research-planning.ts), [exports](https://github.com/A-K-M-Asifuzzaman/MicroFire-Atlas/blob/acb9aaa69486154da657c61469d3a1886e4c12b1/apps/web/lib/research-exports.ts).

Its public `/challenge` page rendered HTTP 200 without page exceptions at 06:04:57 PKT. The opening poses one PMMA habitat question and organizes the answer into nine stages, including uncertainty and the next experiment. This is a more explicit demonstration story than IGNIS's current landing page. That is an editorial assessment of the inspected views, not a measured usability result. The deployed page and pinned repository need not be identical. [Challenge mode](https://microfire-atlas.vercel.app/challenge), [captured view](../../../_raw/competitive_deep_2026-11-15/public_ui/microfire_atlas.png).

The published October 3 artifact contains 100 questions: 95 AI answers, five evidence-only answers and 481 claims. Its original flags include 38 unverified claims, 25 of 307 factual claims with a numeric flag, and three with a unit flag. A later rescore of the same answers reports 16 of 481 unverified and six of 307 numeric flags. The change reflects the checker, not a new model run. Reported latency is 16.076 s median and 30.722 s p95 for that run, not a current provider comparison. [Full results](https://github.com/A-K-M-Asifuzzaman/MicroFire-Atlas/blob/acb9aaa69486154da657c61469d3a1886e4c12b1/apps/web/eval/results-live.json).

Its reported 99.90% citation precision means retained citation identifiers divided by retained plus removed identifiers. It does not mean 99.90% of scientific assertions were independently confirmed. Numeric and unit fidelity count flags on factual claims; the checker has different rules for interpretations and permits some question-derived numbers and arithmetic. These useful transparency artifacts are stronger evidence than a README assertion, but their scores cannot be directly compared to IGNIS's software-test pass count. [Metric definitions](https://github.com/A-K-M-Asifuzzaman/MicroFire-Atlas/blob/acb9aaa69486154da657c61469d3a1886e4c12b1/apps/web/lib/eval-live.ts), [claim checker](https://github.com/A-K-M-Asifuzzaman/MicroFire-Atlas/blob/acb9aaa69486154da657c61469d3a1886e4c12b1/apps/web/lib/ask-core.ts).

The repository supplies an MIT license and explicitly describes the human usability study as not run. IGNIS has neither a chosen original-code license nor independent user evidence yet. [License](https://github.com/A-K-M-Asifuzzaman/MicroFire-Atlas/blob/acb9aaa69486154da657c61469d3a1886e4c12b1/LICENSE), [project description](https://github.com/A-K-M-Asifuzzaman/MicroFire-Atlas/blob/acb9aaa69486154da657c61469d3a1886e4c12b1/README.md).

## FIRE-X

Pinned repository: [`826b0eefcc86b7763978e420668241fe11b5b87f`](https://github.com/Hekimovraiz/FIRE-X/tree/826b0eefcc86b7763978e420668241fe11b5b87f).

FIRE-X documents a broad platform with visualization, SQL retrieval and hardware integration, and supplies MIT-licensed code. Its deployed homepage returned HTTP 503 at 06:05:09 PKT. That is a single availability observation, not proof it is permanently unavailable. Hardware and scientific model performance were not tested. [Project](https://github.com/Hekimovraiz/FIRE-X/blob/826b0eefcc86b7763978e420668241fe11b5b87f/README.md), [live URL](https://fire-x.onrender.com/).

Our independent CSV parsing counted 892 canonical rows and 28 columns. Fifty rows are explicitly labelled partial-gravity interpolations and two are planned LUCI/FM2 entries. Thus its headline count of verified experiments is not a count of 892 homogeneous measured burns. The interpolation labels should be credited rather than suppressed in the comparison. [Canonical CSV](https://github.com/Hekimovraiz/FIRE-X/blob/826b0eefcc86b7763978e420668241fe11b5b87f/data/processed/canonical_experiments.csv), [our count record](../../../_raw/competitive_deep_2026-11-15/additional/artifact_counts.json).

The ingestion code reveals a more consequential distinction from IGNIS. FLEX temperature is assigned one of two constants. BASS pressure is assigned 760 mmHg/101.32 kPa, burn duration can default to 45 s, outcomes are assigned through material/oxygen/display-value rules, and a fan display can populate the airflow field. Those canonical values therefore combine source extraction with assumptions. Our preferred contract keeps missing or uncalibrated measurements explicit. [Pipeline, including FLEX and BASS transformations](https://github.com/Hekimovraiz/FIRE-X/blob/826b0eefcc86b7763978e420668241fe11b5b87f/scripts/pipeline.py#L70).

Its droplet model forms a purported flight-record identifier from user diameter and oxygen inputs; that expression does not look up an original flight row. The citation wrapper selects records by broad keywords and, in the absence of a recognized keyword, uses an unrestricted database condition. It then appends those records to the generated text. This does not establish sentence-level support for the answer. These conclusions follow from the inspected functions; we did not execute an attack or infer intent. [Model citation construction](https://github.com/Hekimovraiz/FIRE-X/blob/826b0eefcc86b7763978e420668241fe11b5b87f/backend/nasa_combustion_engine.py#L208), [citation wrapper](https://github.com/Hekimovraiz/FIRE-X/blob/826b0eefcc86b7763978e420668241fe11b5b87f/backend/citation_rag_engine.py).

## Current IGNIS strengths and gaps

Fresh local GET requests returned HTTP 200 for health, catalogue, the two-sided PMMA condition comparison and the Kapton detector selection. Health reports AI unavailable, no accepted configuration and zero registry errors. [Captured responses](../../../_raw/competitive_deep_2026-11-15/ignis/health.json).

The implemented strengths are bounded: seven Saffire cases in four material/burning-side cohorts; explicit oxygen/pressure tradeoffs without turning proximity into a safety score; 154 flight-marked detector rows with 20 reference rows separated; finite-denominator counts preserving missing cells; and immutable model-assessment revisions with matching Markdown/JSON exports. Existing verification passed 243 offline tests and 17 browser cases with one intentional duplicate skip. Those earlier results were inspected, not rerun for this comparison. [Build status](../BUILD_STATUS.md), [verification](../LOCAL_VERIFICATION_2026-11-15.md), [reproduction guide](../RESEARCH_REUSE.md).

The most significant implementation gap is continuity. `Conditions` and `DetectorLab` invoke a parameterless brief callback. `Workspace.prepareBrief` prepares a pillar preset and tells the user the selected data have separate exports. The selected readings/conditions do not automatically become the assessed evidence. Workspace history is held in React state; no save/reopen or scenario-URL path was found in the inspected implementation. That makes VESTA's receipts, MORS's replay and FlameBound's sharing especially relevant baselines. [Conditions](../../src/components/Conditions.tsx), [detector view](../../src/components/DetectorLab.tsx), [workspace](../../src/components/Workspace.tsx).

Live interpretation is NOT RUN for this comparison and remains disabled. The user is preparing keys; no credential has been designated or examined in this research pass. Publication and code-license decisions remain unchanged. This task made no application-source change, model call, public upload or external contact.

# Competitive update: what IGNIS must actually demonstrate

**Research snapshot:** 15 November 2026, PKT; repository heads captured at 03:59, followed by selected-file inspection. This is a public-source comparison, not a ranking of registered teams or an executed product benchmark. It supersedes the strategic conclusions and current-status statements in the earlier [deep dive](COMPETITOR_DEEP_DIVE_2026-11-15.md); that historical snapshot remains intact.

**Decision:** neither provenance, comparability gates, a Moon atmosphere view, nor Saffire VI alone is a defensible differentiator. IGNIS should compete on the usefulness and correctness of a complete assessment: selecting evidence, preserving measurement meaning, explaining a conditional comparison, revising its assumptions, and exporting a reviewable result. Superiority is a hypothesis to test.

## Corrections to the inherited strategy

1. **“All 24 investigations are table stakes” was an inference, not a judging rule.** The supplied rubric rewards Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation. It does not prescribe a minimum repository, row or investigation count. Broad metadata coverage is useful; deeply curated examples must remain distinguishable from profiles.
2. **The PSI gap is scoped.** “No matching solid row in this acquired PSI subset” does not mean NASA has never run a relevant high-oxygen solid experiment. Current MARGIN and HALO already acknowledge literature beyond that subset. The [physics review](lab/04_physics_completion_2026-11-15.md) adds further relevant primary research.
3. **Saffire VI event identities matter.** The 2024 values 31.0%/54.1 kPa and 30.3%/54.6 kPa describe SIBAL and PMMA respectively; they are not two fabric burns. Calling either “nearest” needs a chosen question, admissible comparison group, axes and metric. Microgravity is not lunar surface gravity.
4. **A declared judgement-based score is not fraudulent physics.** Its sensitivity, missing-data treatment and interpretation should be compared fairly. Conversely, replacing it with Pareto filtering does not remove the choices of axes, evidence eligibility, direction or measurement uncertainty.
5. **IGNIS has acquired primary PSI records and tables.** The earlier “paper-only/no PSI” status is obsolete. Integration into the application is still unfinished. Acquisition, interpretation and a functioning product are distinct milestones.
6. **No source audit proves a win.** We have not measured competitor task success, user effort, latency, scientific error rates or judging outcomes. A repository feature list cannot establish SOTA or a system-performance Pareto frontier.

## A concrete comparison we can already make

The detection specialist obtained NASA's original PSI-101 analyzed-results workbook and recounted, in local code, the finite scatter-channel cells under a selected 2.0 V cutoff: **20/35 Kapton and 18/23 Teflon rows**, matching the reviewed competitor counts. This assistant check supports those descriptive counts. It does not establish actual alarm failures, a detector calibration, independence of all rows, or performance at a different atmosphere.

The same review found that the SAME-R experimental CSV and the workbook each have 109 corresponding campaign rows but differ in one material/test key on each side. Equal totals would conceal that discrepancy. The [detection report](lab/05_detection_completion_2026-11-15.md) records the original cells, file hashes, missingness and unresolved reconciliation. This is a useful candidate demonstration of what IGNIS contributes: preserve the valid descriptive result while identifying exactly which further inference is unsupported.

## What to build and what to measure

Follow the [integrated research decision](RESEARCH_LAB_SYNTHESIS_2026-11-15.md) and [current build order](WIN_PLAN.md). The primary comparator remains a curated spreadsheet containing the same information, as recorded in D-011/D-024. Compare source-supported task completion, incorrectly admitted evidence, useful conditional comparisons, revision correctness, source-verification effort and time. A plain keyword browser and a fixed-weight ranking are secondary baselines; neither replaces that stronger spreadsheet baseline.

An advantage must come from a tested workflow, not from making competitors look careless. Preserve their strongest features in the comparison, publish exact scope and failures, and treat adoption by researchers as a future outcome to earn.

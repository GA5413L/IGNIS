**Subject:** Question on the flow configuration of BASS-II PMMA-rod tests P16/P19 (Link et al. 2018, *Sci. Rep.* 8:120)

Dear Professor Huang,

I am [name, role/affiliation]. I have been studying your 2018 *Scientific Reports* paper on flame spread over PMMA
cylinders, together with NASA's BASS-II Summary Report (NASA/TM-20210011385), for [a data-provenance project /
purpose]. Thank you for publishing the per-test data in the Supplementary Information. It made a detailed
comparison possible.

While matching the supplement's samples to NASA's test log, I found one point I could not reconcile, and I would
be grateful for your guidance.

- **The paper.** It presents samples **B16** (18.2 % O₂) and **B19** (17.5 % O₂), 0.64-cm black rods, as **opposed-flow** tests (Fig. 4, Fig. 6a, Fig. S1).
- **The match to NASA's log.** By diameter, initial O₂ and flow sequence, these appear to correspond to NASA tests **P16** (as-run 93, sample 191m) and **P19** (as-run 94, sample 194m), both run on 22 July 2014.
- **The discrepancy.** In NASA/TM-20210011385, Table A.3 (PDF p.114, printed p.106), both rows show **"Concurrent"** in the column headed "Sample distance". Every other rod test in that table shows "Opposed".
- **Why it may be a log issue.** Chapter 4 of the same report appears to include these tests among the opposed-flow results (the 6.35-mm flow range in Table 4.2 reaches 0.075 m/s, and Fig. 4.1 shows points near 7.5 cm/s). The photographs in your Fig. 4 also look like the opposed-rod geometry. In the appendix, these two tests sit between Olson concurrent-flow tests run on 21 and 22 July.

Could you tell me which of these applies?
1. P16 and P19 were run in **opposed** flow, and the "Concurrent" entries in Table A.3 are a documentation issue.
2. They were run in **concurrent** flow, or under a different flow-direction convention from the one I assumed.
3. B16 and B19 refer to **different burns** from P16 and P19.

If it is easy to answer, three smaller points would also help me record the data correctly:
1. **Units.** The right half of Table S1 (0.64-cm rods) labels V_f×10³ in "(mm/s)", while the left half uses "(cm/s)". Should both be read as cm/s, as Figs 5 and 6a suggest?
2. **B22.** Does sample B22 combine NASA tests P22a and P22b (two burns of specimen 200m)?
3. **P17.** Was sample "P17" a black PMMA rod?

I'm not suggesting any error in your analysis. I'd simply like to describe these tests accurately and cite them
correctly. Thank you very much for your time.
